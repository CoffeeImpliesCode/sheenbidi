## Cryptographic suitability

Not applicable, and stated so rather than left open: this is a text-layout
library. It computes bidirectional levels, runs, script runs and mirror
substitutions, and it ships no cipher, hash, key derivation, random number
generator or other security-relevant primitive. The one table it is built around
that could be mistaken for one — the checked-in UCD lookup data — is public
Unicode character data, carried under the terms above.
